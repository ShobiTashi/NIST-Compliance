# Skypea Corporation 
 
## Company Overview 
All names, customers, and details are fictional and created for the purpose of this project. 

 
## About the Company 
Skypea Corporation is a mid-sized company with approximately 2,400 employees across three countries. It operates two lines of business and a shared corporate function: 

* Skypea Consumer Products manufactures and sells home electronics through retail partners and a direct-to-consumer e-commerce store.
* Skypea Cloud Services operates a subscription software platform (the “Skypea HR Assistant”) used by other businesses to manage employee records, payroll data, and benefits. This is a multi-tenant SaaS product hosted in the public cloud. 
* Corporate and Shared Services provides finance, human resources, legal, and IT for the whole company. 

Annual revenue is roughly split 60 percent Consumer Products and 40 percent Cloud Services, but Cloud Services is the faster-growing line and the strategic priority for the next three years.

 
## Mission and Strategic Priorities
Skypea’s stated mission is to deliver dependable products and services that customers trust with their information. 

Leadership has set three strategic priorities for the current planning cycle: 
1. Grow the Cloud Services subscriber base, with a specific target of winning larger enterprise customers. 
2. Protect customer and employee data as a condition of that growth. 
3. Reduce operational disruption across manufacturing and the SaaS platform. 

 
## Why Skypea Is Adopting the Framework 
Two enterprise prospects have told Skypea Cloud Services that they will not sign without evidence of a mature, well-governed cybersecurity program, and they have asked how Skypea manages third-party and supply chain risk. The board has also asked for a single, defensible way to see and communicate the company’s cybersecurity posture. Skypea has chosen the Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 as that common language and has asked for a first Organizational Profile that can be shown to prospects and to the board.

 
## Data and Systems 
* Customer data (Cloud Services): employee records, payroll figures, and benefits data belonging to Skypea’s business customers and their staff. This is the most sensitive data Skypea holds. 
* Payment data (Consumer Products): the direct-to-consumer e-commerce store processes credit card payments through a third-party payment processor. 
* Corporate data: Skypea employee HR records, financial records, and product intellectual property. 
* Key systems: the Skypea HR Assistant SaaS platform (public cloud), the e-commerce store, an ERP system for manufacturing

 
## Obligations
* Skypea Cloud Services is contractually required by several customers to safeguard the personal data it processes on their behalf and to notify them of security incidents within defined timeframes. 
* Skypea operates in jurisdictions with data protection laws that impose obligations around personal data and breach notification. 
* The Consumer Products e-commerce store must meet payment card security requirements for the card data it handles. 
* Skypea has no sector-specific critical infrastructure obligations.

 
## Governance Facts 
* The board reviews cybersecurity risk on a quarterly basis and has asked for clearer reporting. 
* A newly appointed Chief Information Security Officer is accountable for cybersecurity risk and reports to the Chief Executive Officer. 
* Cybersecurity policy exists but is fragmented across several documents of varying age, and enforcement is inconsistent between the two business lines. 
* Risk appetite has not been formally stated. Leadership describes an informal willingness to accept more risk in Consumer Products than in Cloud Services. 
* Cybersecurity risk is tracked separately from the company’s broader enterprise risk process.

 
## Scoping Guidance from Leadership 
Leadership wants the first Organizational Profile to be useful to the enterprise prospects and the board quickly, rather than perfect and company-wide. They have indicated that the first Profile should center on the part of the business the prospects care about and that holds the most sensitive data. Manufacturing operational technology on the Consumer Products factory floor is acknowledged as important but is explicitly set aside for a later, separate Profile.
