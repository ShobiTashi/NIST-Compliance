## Company Overview 
All names, customers, and details are fictional and created for the purpose of this assessment.

## About the Company 
Skypea Corporation is a mid-sized company with approximately 2,400 employees across three countries. It operates two lines of business and a shared corporate function: 

* Skypea Consumer Products manufactures and sells home electronics through retail partners and a direct-to-consumer e-commerce store.
* Skypea Cloud Services operates a subscription software platform (the “Skypea HR Assistant”) used by other businesses to manage employee records, payroll data, and benefits. This is a multi-tenant SaaS product hosted in the public cloud. 
• Corporate and Shared Services provides finance, human resources, legal, and IT 
for the whole company. 
Annual revenue is roughly split 60 percent Consumer Products and 40 percent Cloud 
Services, but Cloud Services is the faster-growing line and the strategic priority for the next 
three years.

## Security Team and Reporting Lines 
Havana has a small but growing security team led by a Chief Information Security Officer. The CISO owns 
security across the company and reports security posture to the executive team and the board. 
The GRC Analyst reports directly to the CISO. The role coordinates across the company: with 
engineering on how controls are built, with legal on regulatory obligations, with human resources on 
policy acknowledgment and awareness training, and with sales on the security reviews customers require 
before signing.

---
## How Havana Runs Its Security Program 
The CISO approves every security policy before it takes effect, and a Security Steering Committee sets 
annual security objectives and defines how significant decisions are escalated to the executive team. 

Before adopting any new cloud provider or vendor, the team evaluates the exposure that vendor would 
introduce, records the finding in the risk register, and assigns an owner responsible for tracking it to 
resolution. 

Havana responds to detailed security questionnaires from prospective enterprise customers, and prepares 
evidence for an annual independent audit those customers expect before they will sign. 

Every quarter the team reviews the risk register, re-rates each open item on how likely it is and how 
damaging it would be, and reports the most serious items to leadership. 

All employees read and formally acknowledge the acceptable use policy once a year, and new hires 
acknowledge it during onboarding.

## A Note on the Records to Work With 
Havana records are maintained by the people who own the underlying work, and most of them are not 
independently reviewed before use. A risk owner types their own risk level, an activity owner tags their 
own activity, a relationship owner sets their own vendor tier. 

A value recorded in a Havana file is a claim made by a colleague, not an established fact. Part of the work
is checking those claims against the rule that should govern them, and reporting where the two disagree.
